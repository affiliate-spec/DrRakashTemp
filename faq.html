BSG Hospital is a multispeciality hospital with 15 departments: general medicine, general and laparoscopic surgery, orthopedics, pediatric orthopedics, neurosurgery, neurology, urology, cardiology, gastroenterology, pediatrics and neonatology, obstetrics and gynecology, pulmonology, psychiatry, plastic and cosmetic surgery, and critical care and anesthesia.
