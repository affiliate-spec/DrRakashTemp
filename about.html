About the Hospital

A multispeciality hospital and trauma centre for Varanasi and Purvanchal

BSG Hospital is a multispeciality hospital and trauma centre at Chandmari, Varanasi, with a second centre in Bhadohi. It brings 15 specialities, 24×7 emergency care, an advanced ICU, a modular operation theatre, dialysis and an in-house lab together, so patients can get complete treatment in one place.

Who We Serve

Patients and families who need reliable treatment close to home

From a child with fever to an accident in the middle of the night, BSG Hospital is built to handle everyday illness, planned surgery and emergencies.

  • Accident, fracture and trauma patients who need care at any hour.
  • Patients with heart, kidney, stomach, lung, brain or spine problems.
  • Children and newborns who need a child specialist or paediatric orthopaedic care.
  • Patients who need planned surgery, ICU care, dialysis or physiotherapy.
Hospital Strength

Specialists, facilities and emergency care in one hospital

BSG Hospital began with orthopaedic and trauma care under Dr. Rakesh B. Singh and has grown into a multispeciality hospital with specialists across 15 departments.

15 Specialities, from general medicine and surgery to neurosurgery, cardiology and paediatrics.
11 Specialist doctors with MD, MS, DM and MCh qualifications.
24×7 Emergency and trauma care, advanced ICU and ambulance.
Integrated Teams

Why the hospital model matters

A strong recovery experience usually depends on handoffs being managed well. That is why BSG is designed as a coordinated ecosystem rather than a single-consultation brand.

01

Specialists under one roof

Physicians, surgeons and super-specialists work together, so patients do not need to visit different hospitals.

02

Trauma readiness

Accident and trauma patients get treatment 24 hours a day, with operation theatres ready for emergency surgery.

03

Critical care access

An advanced ICU with ventilator support looks after seriously ill patients and patients after major surgery.

04

Recovery support

Physiotherapy, dialysis and follow-up care help patients recover fully after treatment.
