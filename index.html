Why BSG Hospital

A fully equipped hospital for Purvanchal, open day and night

Serious illness and accidents rarely involve just one specialist. At BSG Hospital, emergency doctors, surgeons, physicians and the ICU team work in the same building, so decisions are faster and patients are not shifted from place to place.
